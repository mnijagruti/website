Digitron Automation - Your Trusted Partner in Industrial Automation

Digitron Automation is a Pune-based company specializing in industrial process automation and IoT solutions. With our extensive experience and technical expertise, we deliver cutting-edge automation systems that enhance operational efficiency and productivity.

Our comprehensive product range includes data loggers, display systems, and IoT-enabled devices that help businesses monitor, control, and optimize their industrial processes. We pride ourselves on delivering reliable solutions that withstand the rigors of industrial environments while providing accurate data and control capabilities.

We are committed to providing innovative automation solutions that drive efficiency and productivity for our clients across various industries.

  • High-quality industrial automation products
  • Customized IoT solutions for specific needs
  • Nationwide service network with prompt support

Our team of experienced professionals works closely with clients to understand their unique requirements and deliver solutions that exceed expectations. With a focus on quality and reliability, we ensure that our products perform consistently in demanding industrial environments.
